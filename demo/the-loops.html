The loops · what keeps running without me

A library of the things that keep moving.

Loops are the repeatable jobs that run through the campus, with a human decision still visible at the end. This is where to find what is active, what happens next, and how to ask for a change.

Newsletter loop

active

A four-day editorial loop for one useful, current post: drafted, illustrated, sent to you for review, then published only when you approve it.

  • Next runMonday · 09:00
  • Owneryou · the studio
  • Human gateReview and publish
  1. 01PietClaude, scheduled taskFinds a timely angle and writes the unpublished draft.
  2. 02CodexillustrationCreates a cover in the newsletter's established style.
  3. 03Mailerreview emailSends you the private preview and three clear actions: approve, change, drop.
  4. 04YouapprovalRead it, ask for a change, or publish. Nothing goes out until you do.

Follow-ups loop

building

A twice-weekly pass over conversations that have gone quiet, producing suggested replies you can send or bin. Not yet trusted to run unattended.

  • Next runnot scheduled yet
  • Owneryou · the studio
  • Human gateEvery message is yours to send
  1. 01NellClaude, scheduled taskReads the inbox and the meeting notes for threads that need a nudge.
  2. 02NelldraftDrafts one suggested reply per thread, with the reason it thinks a reply is due.
  3. 03YousendSend, edit or bin each one. Nell never sends.

How to change one: use its name. Share this page with the person or agent doing the work and say "update the newsletter loop". This page is a register written for humans, not the scheduler itself; the schedule lives in the rota, the runs leave receipts on the board.
